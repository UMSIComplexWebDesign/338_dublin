Welcome to Dublin

Thirteen U-M students. One city. Three weeks of talks, walking tours, museum visits, Irish dance lessons(!!), and conversations that connected our coursework on accessibility, information, and design to the streets of Dublin.

Browse our shared reflections below, or jump straight to an individual student site.
