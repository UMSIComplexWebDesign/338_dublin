Summary

This museum looks at the history of Irish emigration, its causes, and the effects that can still be seen today.

Quick Facts

Location
CHQ Building, Custom House Quay, Dublin 1
Focus
Irish emigration — causes, journeys, and the global Irish diaspora
Layout
Single-level linear path with wide doorways

Accessibility Checklist

  • Exhibits and Objects: Almost every exhibit or item of interest was placed at a level accessible to all users, including written descriptions, screens, and passport stamps. Nothing was difficult for wheelchair users to access.
  • Labels and Text: Very accessible. Every text was illuminated, making it easy to read for all viewers. That said, braille was not present.
  • Audio Visuals and Interactive: Many areas included both text and audio description, most apparent in rooms with emigrant actors narrating their stories. An audio guide was available at the beginning of the tour.
  • Pathway and Navigation: Arguably the museum’s most accessible feature. The entirety of the museum was on the same level. Elevators were present at the start and end of the tour, and the pathways were incredibly wide.
  • Seating and Furniture: Almost every room had at least somewhere to sit — a bench, stool, or chair. There was never a shortage of seating.
  • Safety and Content: Incredibly well lit, with a simple linear path and clearly marked fire exits. Very few tripping hazards for elderly guests or those with movement disabilities.
