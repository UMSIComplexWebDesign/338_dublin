Summary

We did both the Guinness Storehouse tour and the Stoutie experience. The Storehouse tour was self-guided through the building, centered around an atrium shaped like a Guinness glass, and ended with a Guinness at the top of the building overlooking the city. The Stoutie added an extra Guinness with a photo laminated onto the top of the foam.

Quick Facts

Location
St. James’s Gate, Dublin 8
Format
Self-guided tour, 7 floors
Highlight
Gravity Bar with panoramic views
Add-on
Stoutie — photo printed on Guinness foam

Accessibility Features

Accessible-only lift

The Storehouse offers an accessible-only lift, reducing wait times for those unable to use the stairs.

Audio guides & ISL tours

Audio guides are available for guests whose primary language is not English, and the Storehouse also offers International Sign Language tours.

Low signage

Signs were often placed low enough for wheelchair users to read comfortably.

Description of the Guinness harp

The plaque notes the famous Downhill Harp, dating to 1702, which was purchased by Guinness in 1963 to ensure its preservation.

Accessibility Concerns

Low-contrast text on glass

At various points throughout the self-guided tour, writing on glass was incredibly low contrast — difficult to read for visually-impaired guests.

Patterned floor lighting

The floor was often illuminated with patterns, which could be disorienting for visually-impaired visitors.

Dimly-lit walkways

A majority of the walkways at the start of the tour were dimly lit, which can be challenging for visitors with low vision.

Fast-moving video

While the tour was immersive, the large video screens could be overwhelming for guests sensitive to flashing lights or fast-moving visuals.

Each Floor in the Storehouse Experience

Lower Ground Floor
Entry point — tickets, group entrance, and audio guides.
Ground Floor
The core ingredients of Guinness — water, barley, and hops — plus the retail store.
Floor 1
The roasting and brewing processes, the cooperage, and an exhibition space.
Floor 2
The sensory experience of the tasting rooms, plus the Arrol Suite.
Floor 3
A dedicated cinema room and various advertising exhibits.
Floor 4
The Guinness Academy, the Connoisseur Bar, and the Stoutie experience.
Floor 5
Dining and drinks at the 1837 Bar & Brasserie and Arthur’s Bar.
Floor 7
The Gravity Bar, with panoramic views of Dublin while you enjoy a pint.
